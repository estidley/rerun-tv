# Built-in channel guide

This is the complete catalog of Rerun's fixed channels and generated channel families. These are local programming presets, **not live feeds or content sources**. Every channel draws only from the Plex libraries you select.

Numbers are preferred defaults. Rerun preserves personalized numbering and moves a generated channel to a free number when necessary. Display names are fictional or thematic; no affiliation with a broadcaster or content provider is implied.

## Minimum programming

Ordinary stations use the selected **Low (4 hours)**, **Medium (12 hours, the default)**, or **High (24 hours)** threshold. Special minimums listed above use the higher of their own threshold and the selected preset.

A per-channel override can lower or raise that requirement, but even **No minimum** requires a nonempty eligible pool. Channels without enough programming remain dormant or absent from the guide. None of these presets adds media: every listing still comes from your own selected libraries.
